👓 Eyeglass Rx Toolkit 👓

Welcome to my Rx Conversion Toolkit! After many years working as an optician, I've spent lots of time converting prescriptions and calculating lens powers in different meridians. That inspired me to come up with a program that can do that work for me!

What's included?

Cyl Transposition

Transpose a single vision prescription from plus cyl to minus cyl or vice versa.

Power at 90/180

Find the power of a single vision prescription at a specified meridian.

Multifocal to Single Vision

Convert a multifocal prescription into a single vision distance, intermediate, or reading prescription.
